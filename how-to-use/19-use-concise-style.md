# Use concise response style

Use this when you want shorter replies across a conversation while keeping the important details.

## Turn it on

Invoke `concise` in your agent’s skill picker or supported shortcut. Shortcut syntax depends on the host. Or send:

> Use concise style for replies in this conversation. Put the answer first and remove filler and repetition. Preserve key facts, caveats, exact technical details, and necessary questions. Do not skip analysis or verification. Continue until I ask for normal detail.

## Adjust or turn it off

Ask for more detail on a specific answer, or say:

> Turn off concise style and return to normal detail.

A request for a detailed artifact or explanation takes precedence for that response.

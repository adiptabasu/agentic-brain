# Write or edit prose

Use this to draft or revise content for clarity, tone, audience, or flow. For a standalone formatted document, use [Create a document from a brief or template](16-create-a-document.md). For codebase documentation, use [Write or update technical documentation](10-document-a-change.md).

## Draft

> Read skills/writing/SKILL.md and follow it. Write a [format] about [topic] for [audience]. The reader should [takeaway]. Use [sources or facts]. Aim for a [tone] tone and [length]. Do not add facts beyond the supplied material; flag gaps that matter.

Example:

> Read skills/writing/SKILL.md and follow it. Write a short explanation of how password managers reduce reuse risk for a general audience. Use the attached security guidance, keep it direct and practical, and distinguish general advice from claims specific to our product.

## Revise

> Read skills/writing/SKILL.md and follow it. Rewrite this for [audience]. Make it [clearer/shorter/more conversational/more formal]. Preserve its meaning, facts, and caveats. Remove generic filler and repetition; do not invent examples or expertise.

## Review

> Summarize any changes to meaning, list claims you could not verify, and point out anything that needs subject-matter review. Do not claim a person wrote or reviewed this unless they did.

The goal is credible, natural writing grounded in context, not fabricated experience or authority.
